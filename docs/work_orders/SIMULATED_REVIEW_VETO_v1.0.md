# Restrictive simulated review veto

R-TM-019 requires simulated oversight with accountable review decisions. R-MD-040 (GOV-003) asks for override direction, rationale and downstream outcome. R-MD-058 (OPS-003) counts overrides as part of approval burden. This WP06 increment uses the accepted WP05 dispatch boundary and WP12 verified measurement paths. The owner authorized the public development draft after the exact PR #22 merge. D-017 and DEC-038 govern its release.

## Decision and controls

Give the harness a closed, optional policy on a governed guarded-review configuration. The two settings are `retain_approval` and `veto_approval`. An opportunity occurs once for an admitted request reaching a timely planned approval. Assess the bound request before recording its terminal review decision. Expiry, a configured ordinary denial, lack of a response and pre-admission refusals cannot produce a veto opportunity. The policy belongs to the scenario and its rationale is prescribed. The defender cannot choose it or read a future decision in an admission brief.

A simulated veto changes the planned approval to denial and records the mission-continuity rationale. Denied requests end there. The matched retain control records its planned response and follows the usual fresh governor check, dispatch and verification. An assessment grants no execution authority. Record the request digest, referral, subject, assessment tick, original and final response, direction and rationale. Downstream evidence belongs to that request. Preserve actual authorization failures, execution failures, verification and rollback outcomes when observed.

## Measurement and replay

Use the existing `pacd-sim metrics --approval-burden` episode, pinned plan and pinned register paths. Count exercised vetoes for OPS-003. For GOV-003 publish timely planned approval opportunities, the exercised fraction, and the observed decisions and downstream events. A modeled control with observed opportunities can have a measured rate of zero. Zero opportunities give a null rate even when the count is zero. Runs produced under earlier contracts retain their unsupported null override count. Incomplete, failed, timed-out and invalid attempts keep their existing status and null measurements.

Reconstruct the assessment, timing, binding and downstream lifecycle from the governed inputs during verification. A self-consistent ledger and a valid receipt alone cannot authenticate a substituted decision. Preserve historical scenario discovery, configuration identities, v1/v2 binding and released metric digests.

## Acceptance

1. Compare governed retain and veto fixtures on identical synthetic event inputs and defender conditions using development seeds 424242 and 424243. Pin each configuration, code revision, seed registry and invocation before launch. Include historical, zero-opportunity and unlaunched controls.
2. Verify that an exercised veto produces one bound denial and zero dispatches for its request. Check that retaining approval still requires current authority. Exercise expiry, ordinary denial, absent response, zero admissions and altered evidence. Compare public episode, plan and register reads.
3. Reproduce full suites in both frozen environments, replay prior releases, check source reconciliation and submit the exact head for independent review. Record observed results in the PR evidence and handoff.
